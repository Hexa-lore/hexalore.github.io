Finally got my weight checked and realized that I'm already **42 kilograms?!** Wow. I don’t know if that’s completely accurate, but if it is, then it’s an amazing achievement.

From **53 kilos to 42 kilos** — that’s definitely a milestone to celebrate. I have PCOS, which makes me more insulin resistant. That means I have to make certain changes in my lifestyle: move with intention and exercise with function.

But honestly, I did neglect my diet and movement for a while due to some events. I think I was just really overwhelmed and tired.

Anyway, it’s another random day, and I just want to rate the **Dubai chewy cookies** I’ve tried so far.

I’ve only tried four so far, and two of them were from the same shop.  
My rating is based on:
- how marshmallowy the texture is  
- the pistachio and kanafe filling  
- and the price

### Top 1 — Cosmo Cookies
This one tastes the closest to a marshmallow filled with pistachio and crunchy bits. It only cost **130 pesos per piece**, and the portion size was really good.  
⭐⭐⭐ / 5

### Top 2 — Lamela Bakery
This one is more **mochi-like than marshmallowy**, but overall it's still worth it for the price and the filling. It cost **185 pesos**, but the filling really earns its spot at number two.  

By the way, we got **two flavors** from this shop: pistachio and chocolate.  
⭐⭐ / 5

### Top 3 — Ti Amo Tiramisu
I ranked this last mainly because it costs **175 pesos**. I couldn’t really taste the marshmallow, and it was a bit too nutty for me. It also had a noticeable aftertaste.

Overall, it’s not bad. The cocoa is slightly bitter. I do like dark chocolate, but this one was **a little too bitter**, and it didn’t work well with the aftertaste.  
⭐ / 5
